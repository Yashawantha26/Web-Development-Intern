Hoysala Restaurant was born from a deep love for Karnataka's culinary heritage — the same traditions that once graced the courts of the great Hoysala kings. We bring those forgotten recipes back to life, using ingredients sourced from local farmers in Hassan district.

Founded by Yashawantha H M in the heart of Hoovinahally, our kitchen is guided by one belief: food is not merely nourishment — it is culture, memory, and belonging.

From the smoky earthen pots to the stone-ground masalas, every dish carries the soul of the land. We are proud to serve the community of Hassan with warmth, authenticity, and a touch of royal grandeur.
